    Black Field & Bleed

    Open 1 in fullscreen. Look for light at the edges, and move your head to tell IPS glow from bleed.

    Dead & Stuck Pixels

    Open 3 and click through each solid color. Every subpixel should light up evenly.

    Near-Black Banding

    Open 2 at 2%–5%. Look for vertical stripes or cloudy patches.

    Text & Motion

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Why not just use a test video?

Video codecs discard subtle near-black detail and introduce banding and blocking — exactly the artifacts you are trying to find. Canvas patterns are drawn losslessly at your device’s native resolution.

Can a browser detect my panel’s real bit depth or HDR support?

Only approximately. Browsers report a color depth (usually 24 bits, i.e. 8 bits per channel) and whether the output is wide-gamut or HDR. That describes the signal path, not the panel itself, so PanelProbe labels these values as browser-reported.

How do I read the gamma test?

Enable 1:1 pixel mapping and view it in fullscreen. Each tile surrounds a solid gray patch with fine black/white lines. Step back or squint: the patch that blends into its surround matches your display’s gamma. Most content targets 2.2.

Does it install anything or send data anywhere?

No. It is a static site with no analytics, cookies, or third-party requests — fonts included. Preferences such as theme and mute are saved in your browser’s local storage.

Is it safe for people with photosensitive epilepsy?

Some stress tests flash rapidly. The studio shows a warning before use, the strobe test is excluded from Test All, and flashing tests stop automatically. If you are photosensitive, avoid the Stress category.

Can the Stuck Pixel Reviver fix dead pixels?

Sometimes it helps a stuck LCD subpixel that is lit or partially responsive. A pixel that stays completely dark is usually dead and cannot be revived by software — check your warranty’s dead-pixel policy.
